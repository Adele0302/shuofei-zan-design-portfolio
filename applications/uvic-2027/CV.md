## Research experience

### Ceramic form modelling, preferences, and human evaluation | 2024–2026

- Standardised and analysed **225 ceramic vessel profiles** (165 historical and 60 contemporary). The first two PCA components represented **93.24%** of observed profile variation.
- Compared Ridge and RBF-SVR prediction models and distilled **five interpretable proportion rules**, supported by geometric characteristics of rim, neck, belly, and overall form.
- Conducted a separate **30-participant, 48-question two-alternative forced-choice study** comparing rule-generated shapes against weaker baselines and highly rated real examples.
- Built a **three-condition browser-based design-support interface**, tested with **18 participants**; logged acceptance, rejection, undo, time, and revision behaviours. Additional guidance increased task time without a statistically detected improvement in final rule alignment.

### Ceramic heritage and material-appearance studies | 2024–2026

- Interpreted and visualised **XRF, XRD, Raman, SEM-EDS and XPS** results for historical glaze-colour, microstructure and authenticity studies.
- Contributed to studies involving Ru-system, Longquan, Yaozhou and Ge-ware ceramics, including comparisons between Tang-period and modern yellow-glazed objects.
- Collaborated on literature synthesis, quantitative comparison, figures and publication manuscripts. This is material-characterisation experience, **not a claim of completed haptics experiments**.
