## Research experience

### Interactive Guidance and Behavioural Evaluation | 2025–2026

- Built a browser-based within-subject study with 18 participants and three guidance conditions: unguided design, rule cues, and refinement cues with target information.
- Logged recommendation acceptance, rejection, undo, revisions and task time to examine both design outcomes and responses to computational suggestions.
- Guided conditions took longer on average (75.9–77.6 seconds versus 53.3 seconds unguided); rule-distance differences were not statistically significant, and recommendation acceptance was 58.3% in each guided condition.

### Interpretable Ceramic Form Modelling and Preference Evaluation | 2024–2026

- Quantified 225 ancient and modern vessel outlines (165 ancient; 60 modern), standardising contour descriptions for statistical comparison.
- PCA explained 85.12% in PC1 and 93.24% cumulatively in PC1–PC2; compared Ridge and RBF-SVR preference models using cross-validated rank correlation.
- Translated observed model relationships into five interpretable design-proportion rules that can be inspected and tested as design guidance.
- Validated generated forms in a separate 30-participant, 48-question 2AFC study: rule-guided forms beat weaker baselines, but not highly rated real examples.

### HoberGlow — Parametric Mechanism and Design Prototyping | 2025–2026

- Modelled a transformable scissor-linkage luminaire in Rhino/Grasshopper, exploring kinematic constraints and opening/closing configurations.
- Used a reduced Karamba3D structural proxy and exploratory optical comparisons to compare configurations and document modelling assumptions.
- Documented motion states and design alternatives to communicate mechanism constraints and prototype trade-offs.

### Ceramic Materials, Glaze Colour and Heritage Evidence | 2024–2026

- Contributed to collaborative studies of historical Chinese ceramics, interpreting XRF, XRD, Raman, SEM-EDS and XPS evidence as relevant to each research question.
- Connected compositional, phase and microstructural evidence with visual comparison across Ru-system, Longquan, Yaozhou and Ge ware; also examined Tang-period yellow glazes and Jin-period moon-white glaze.
- Linked measurement provenance and visual comparison to evidence-based interpretations of glaze colour and authenticity; reconstructed 60 Ru- and Yaozhou-related forms in Rhino.

### Urban Second Foyer — User-centred Service Design | 2026

- Used stakeholder analysis, user journeys and service blueprinting to translate outdoor workers' rest needs into a public-space support concept.

### Rest Light — Affective Interaction Concept | 2026

### Airport Tower Operator Seat — Ergonomic Design | 2022–2023

### Shared Kitchen — Community Service Design | 2025–2026
