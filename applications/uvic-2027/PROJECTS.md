# Selected research and design projects

The following cases are selected for the **Happiness Cultivating Technologies for Work** doctoral opportunity. They distinguish implemented experiments, design concepts and research directions; no unpublished outcome is presented as a completed result.

## 1. Interpretable design-support and human evaluation

**Research problem.** Can a machine-derived rule help people make and revise design decisions without taking away control?

**Evidence and methods.** Based on **225 ceramic profiles** (165 historical, 60 contemporary), I measured form proportions, used PCA, compared Ridge/SVR predictors and formulated five interpretable design rules. A separate **30-participant 2AFC** study tested design-form judgements. An **18-participant three-condition browser study** tracked user actions including acceptance, rejection, undo, revisions and completion time.

**What this taught me.** More elaborate guidance does not automatically make design work better: longer task time was observed without a statistically detected improvement in rule alignment. I therefore want to evaluate process experience, user autonomy and meaningful exploration rather than efficiency alone.

**Public documentation:** [Interpretable Form Modeling and Human Evaluation](https://github.com/Adele0302/interpretable-form-modeling-human-evaluation)

**My contribution.** Form-data processing, baseline comparison, rule interpretation, study design and analytical interpretation.

## 2. Urban Second Foyer — Services and experience for outdoor workers

**Design challenge.** Create a more useful resting and support environment for outdoor workers by understanding the circumstances in which they use public space.

**Work performed.** Stakeholder analysis, persona development, user journey mapping, and service-blueprint definition of spatial/service touchpoints. Concept development connected design responses to realistic tasks and user needs.

**Relevance to VIXI.** Contextual inquiry and service design methods can help identify what meaningful, positive experiences look like in everyday work—not just whether a prototype functions.

**Outcome.** Provincial First Prize, China Good Creativity Competition (2026). This is a team/service-design project, **not a clinical wellbeing intervention or a completed happiness evaluation**.

**Visual portfolio:** [Design & Research Portfolio](https://github.com/Adele0302/shuofei-zan-design-portfolio#view-the-portfolio)

## 3. HoberGlow — Transformable lighting and embodied experience

**Design challenge.** Connect visible physical transformation, mechanism constraints and lighting feedback in an articulated product.

**Implementation.** Rhino/Grasshopper parameterised scissor-linkage movement and opening/closing configurations; a reduced Karamba3D proxy helped compare candidate configurations. Exploratory optics indicated design directions, not photometric certification.

**Relevance to VIXI.** A grounding in tangible interaction, prototype iteration and physically meaningful feedback can inform the development of creative technologies.

**Public demonstration/documentation:** [HoberGlow Parametric Mechanism](https://github.com/Adele0302/hoberglow-parametric-mechanism-fea)

**Disclosure.** Public assets are limited to materials the applicant is authorised to share. No protected partner CAD models are claimed or provided.

## 4. Rest Light — Affective product-interaction concept

**Design challenge.** Explore how product form, ambient light and AI-mediated responses could support reflective emotional experiences.

**Work performed.** Scenario design and conceptual interaction flows, including physical and digital feedback decisions.

**Relevance.** Early-stage affective and experiential design work; **not yet a validated mental-health or happiness intervention**.

**Outcome.** Provincial Third Prize, China Good Creativity Competition (2026).

---

**Research focus for the PhD.** I am interested in studying how creativity-support technologies can preserve autonomy and exploration while contributing to positive emotions, meaning and relatedness. This is a *proposed doctoral direction*, not an empirically demonstrated finding of the projects above.
